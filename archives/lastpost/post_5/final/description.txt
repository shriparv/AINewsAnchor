📰 The Intersection of Encryption and AI
👉 Bruce Schneier's 2010 column for Dark Reading argued that cryptography alone cannot address modern network security challenges like denial-of-service attacks and data theft, reflecting on how his influential work was used by NSA cryptographers in the 1990s during cryptography's transition from academic niche to mainstream engineering discipline.

📰 Lithuanian startup launches open-source network to detect Shahed-type drones
👉 A Lithuanian startup has launched an open-source acoustic drone detection network using volunteers' Android phones to identify Shahed-type drones by analyzing their low-frequency engine sounds. The system triangulates drone locations when multiple devices detect the same signal, with participation from technology and security experts across Lithuania.

📰 Polestar Faces a Ban on Selling Its EVs in the US
👉 Polestar will be banned from selling its 2027 and later electric vehicle models in the U.S. due to concerns over Chinese-connected technology, with the company confirming it has no plans to resume U.S. sales, including for the upcoming Polestar 7. The Swedish automaker, owned by China's Geely through Volvo, will continue selling existing inventory and supporting current customers in the U.S. while shifting manufacturing to Europe.

📰 This Week in Security: Arch AUR, Steam Marketplace, WordPress All Face Issues, Taco-Themed Coding, and Mythos Makes National News
👉 Starting June 11, 2026, over 1,500 packages in the Arch User Repository (AUR) were compromised by malware that exploited the repository's package adoption process, installing malicious NPM packages designed to steal credentials, tokens, and cryptocurrency wallets from browsers and Electron-based applications. The malware employs advanced cloaking techniques including process renaming and systemd services to maintain persistence while targeting popular applications like Slack, Discord, and Signal.
