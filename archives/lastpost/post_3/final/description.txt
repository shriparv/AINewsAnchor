📰 How Android Gamers Are Now Getting Desktop-Level Frame Rates
👉 Android gamers can now enjoy desktop-level frame rates with the introduction of lossless frame generation, allowing for smoother gameplay and improved visual quality. This feature, previously exclusive to high-end gaming systems, enables mid-range Android devices to handle demanding titles like Cyberpunk 2077.

📰 AI memory crunch will make smartphones more expensive
👉 AI memory chip demand, driven by data centers, has led to a shortage in Dynamic Random Access Memory (DRAM) and NAND flash memory used in smartphones and PCs. As a result, phone makers will have to reduce the amount of memory or increase prices, with some devices shipping with less RAM.

📰 Samsung drops advanced packaging tech from next-gen Exynos 2700 processor
👉 Samsung is abandoning its advanced Fan-Out Wafer Level Packaging (WLP) technology for its next-generation mobile processor, Exynos 2700, due to high manufacturing costs. The decision comes as Samsung balances performance gains against financial returns. WLP was previously used in Exynos processors since the Exynos 2400, offering improved thermal management but with increased costs.

📰 I love Pixel phones, but the bugs are getting worse
👉 A long-time user of Google Pixel phones reports that despite their positive qualities, such as smooth performance and responsive interfaces, the devices are plagued by persistent bugs, which have worsened over time. The issue has been a recurring problem for years, even after the Pixel 6's major reboot in 2021.

📰 The Lenovo Yoga 7a is gorgeous, but AMD's Ryzen AI 7 445 feels like a downgrade
👉 The Lenovo Yoga 7a features a sleek design and premium feel, but is let down by AMD's Ryzen AI 7 445 processor, which feels underpowered and inefficient. This top-spec model is not worth choosing over its Intel counterpart, despite its excellent build quality, generous RAM, and gorgeous OLED touch-capable display.
