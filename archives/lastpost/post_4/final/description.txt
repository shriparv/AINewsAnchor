📰 The submariner-turned-top Army tech boss said the hardest thing about modernizing isn't the new tools — it's the people
👉 The U.S. Army's former chief information officer, Leonel Garciga, emphasized that modernizing the military is hindered not by technology but by resistance to change within its workforce and institutional culture. He advocated for rapid deployment of new tools, prioritizing user experience and accepting risks to foster innovation and adaptation. Garciga's approach focused on breaking traditional bureaucratic barriers to enable faster experimentation and implementation across the force. His tenure ended last Friday, marking the close of a three-year effort to accelerate digital transformation in the Army.

📰 'We Still Can't See Dark Matter. But What If We Can Hear It?'
👉 Scientists may have accidentally detected dark matter in 2019 and are now exploring the possibility of detecting it through sound rather than visual means. New research published in Physical Review Letters investigates this alternative approach to finding the elusive substance that makes up most of the universe's mass.

📰 Russian spies are aggressively seeking Western technology as sanctions bite, officials say
👉 Russian intelligence agencies are intensifying efforts to steal Western technology and defense secrets as sanctions strain the country's wartime economy. Moscow is using fake companies, middlemen, and cyber spies to acquire advanced machinery, research, and dual-use technology that could enhance its military capabilities. European officials report increased targeting of defense industries and high-end research, including technology for civilian use that could be integrated into Russian weapons systems. The campaign reflects Russia's push to maintain technological parity with the West despite economic pressures.

📰 NASA’s Next-Gen Processor Is 500 Times More Powerful Than Current Space Chips
👉 NASA is testing a next-generation processor that delivers 500 times more computing power than current space chips, enabling real-time spacecraft responses and faster data processing. The radiation-hardened chip supports NASA's goals for autonomous missions and scientific discovery as the agency prepares for lunar and Martian exploration. Developed through a partnership, the new multicore system is fault-tolerant and designed to withstand extreme space conditions. Initial tests began in February with promising results, marking a significant upgrade from older, reliable but slower processors.

📰 Republicans Claim Anti-Data Center Movement Is a Chinese Psy-Op
👉 Republican lawmakers are urging the FBI to investigate whether anti-AI sentiment in the U.S. is part of a Chinese influence campaign. Representatives Brett Guthrie, John Joyce, and Bob Latta cited reports from the Bitcoin Policy Institute and Power the Future, which allege China is working to slow U.S. AI development. The lawmakers specifically pointed to Senator Bernie Sanders' call for a moratorium on AI data centers and his hosting of a panel featuring Chinese academics as evidence of this influence. The letter requests a briefing by June 18, 2026.
