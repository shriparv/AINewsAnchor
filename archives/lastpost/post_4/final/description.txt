📰 Starship carries Starlink satellites on key SpaceX test mission
👉 SpaceX successfully conducted a key test mission, carrying Starlink satellites on its starship. The mission marks a significant step forward in the development of the company's satellite constellation.

📰 Starlink's mobile ambitions could run into a wall of steel and concrete
👉 Starlink's ambition to become a mainstream mobile carrier by 2027 faces skepticism from analyst Benedict Evans, who highlights the challenges of providing urban coverage, citing the difficulty of signals being blocked by steel and concrete structures.

📰 SpaceX Agrees to Remove Temporary Gas Turbines at Colossus Data Center By 2027
👉 SpaceX has agreed to remove the temporary gas turbines powering part of its Colossus AI data center complex in Mississippi, with the goal of completing the removal by July 2027. The company will begin the process by August, as it brings a permanent 1.2 gigawatt natural-gas power plant online.

📰 Tesla built a Starlink internet dish into its Cybercab robotaxi
👉 Tesla has integrated a Starlink internet dish into its Cybercab robotaxi, enabling riders to access high-speed internet for streaming and productivity. This integration aims to address gaps in cellular coverage and support the company's expansion of its self-driving robotaxi service.

📰 Elon Musk says SpaceX's AI revenue will outpace all its other products by next month
👉 Elon Musk predicts that AI revenue at SpaceX will surpass all other revenue by September, with significant growth expected in the fourth quarter.
